ACTIVE · PART SPONSORED BY SANZAF

Codegirls South Africa

CodeGirls gives women access to practical coding education and business learning. Phase 1 covers web development, including HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The advanced pathway focuses on Python, SQL and Django. AFPPM built the South African programme from zero into successive cohorts and manages delivery at TSIBA Business School in Pinelands.

In the 2025 intake, 58 learners enrolled, 44 completed the first phase and 31 passed. Fifteen of the 58 were employed or self-employed. The current Cape Town cohort is active, with a once-off SANZAF sponsorship supporting 15 women. These outcomes give partners a foundation to back continued delivery and expansion.
